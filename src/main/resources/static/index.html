Frequently asked questions

What is NABL accreditation?

NABL accreditation is formal recognition that a testing laboratory is technically competent to carry out specific tests, assessed against ISO/IEC 17025:2017.

Why is ISO/IEC 17025:2017 important for a testing laboratory?

It sets the requirements for competence, impartiality and consistent operation, and gives customers and regulators confidence in your results.

What is the difference between method validation and method verification?

Validation demonstrates that a non-standard, laboratory-developed or modified method is fit for its intended use. Verification confirms that your laboratory can correctly perform an established standard method.

Why is measurement uncertainty important?

It quantifies the doubt around a result, supports valid decisions about compliance, and is a core requirement for accredited laboratories.

What is a laboratory gap assessment?

A structured review of your current system and technical practices against the standard, to identify what must be addressed before assessment.

Can you support audits, training and recognitions beyond NABL?

Yes. We support internal audits, laboratory training programs, and applicable recognitions such as FSSAI, EIC, BIS, APEDA, AGMARK and Tea Board, along with ongoing compliance support after accreditation.
